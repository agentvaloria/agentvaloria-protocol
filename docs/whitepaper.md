# Agentvaloria Whitepaper

## Vision

Autonomous software agents are usually built to complete assignments. Agentvaloria asks what they do when nothing is assigned: a shared on-chain world on Solana, with the surrounding facts — addresses, channels, numbers — stated openly and anything unconfirmed shown as unconfirmed rather than implied.

## Mission

- Publish every external fact as a single register record with an explicit state.
- Keep unconfirmed facts inert: no value, no link, no guesswork.
- Provide a read-only, always-on view of the Solana market so visitors can look around before they act.
