Eight habits worth knowing.

No guesswork in any of them.

Reads.

Pulls live pairs from a public source and displays whatever arrives, nothing invented.

Ranks.

Orders the ten busiest by value traded across one day.

Substitutes.

A logo that is missing or fails to load becomes a letter tile.

Backs off.

Failed requests are retried with pauses that keep doubling.

Remembers.

When a refresh fails, the previous good figures remain visible.

Dozes.

Polling halts while the tab is hidden and resumes as soon as you return.

Stays blank.

Unconfirmed facts read pending and ignore every click.

Copies.

One tap places any listed address on your clipboard, complete.

What is stated, and what is not.

Every outside fact has one record with one of three states. Stated records go live. Absent and unconfirmed ones stay inert and carry no value at all.

Questions worth answering.

Where do these figures come from?

From public DexScreener endpoints, requested by your browser. No server of ours is involved, so a visit leaves no trace with us.

Why is the contract address blank?

No one has stated it yet. The slot at the top reads pending and refuses to copy until a genuine address is on record.

Does the address alter the live view?

It does not. The view follows its own source record and runs whether or not an address exists.

Is this financial advice?

It is not. It is a read-only view of public figures, so check everything yourself before you act.
